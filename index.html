SM9 Forge

GM/T 0044 · GB/T 38635 · ISO/IEC 14888-3 · ISO/IEC 18033-5

Extracts an identity key by inverting in the exponent, then runs SM9's signature, key exchange and encryption against the worked examples printed in the standard itself.

WHY IT MATTERS

When your name is your public key, somebody has to mint the private one. SM9 gives that authority two master keys rather than one, so it can both read your mail and sign in your name — and the arithmetic that makes this convenient is the same arithmetic that makes a passing signature check prove less than it looks.

Not production cryptography — a teaching demo. Everything runs in your browser; no key material leaves this page or is stored anywhere.
